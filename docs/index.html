Fister VR

Play fister.fun in your Quest 2 headset straight from the Quest Browser. No PC, SteamVR or extensions needed. You save a bookmark that adds an Enter VR button to the running game.

Unofficial fan mod, v0.7.6. Not made by or affiliated with the Fister developers.

Set it up once
_□×
  1. Open this page in the Quest Browser inside your headset, then choose your settings and press Copy bookmarklet.
  2. Bookmark any page (the star in the address bar). Open Bookmarks, edit that bookmark, rename it Fister VR, and paste over its URL. The pasted text starts with javascript:.
  3. Go to fister.fun. You can start from the main menu; you don't need to be in a match.
  4. Open Bookmarks and tap Fister VR. Two buttons appear bottom-left: Enter VR and VR Info.
  5. Press Enter VR. The game's menus float in front of you; point your right hand and pull the trigger to click. When a match starts you're placed in your avatar's head. Click the left stick in to recenter.

If something's off
_□×
  • No buttons after tapping the bookmark: the page wasn't finished loading. Wait a moment and tap it again. You can stay in VR through menus, loading screens and between rounds.
  • Something stops working: right after it happens, hold both grips for a second to open the debug console on the menu panel, or leave VR and press VR Info. The top lines show what the mod thinks is happening, such as whether you're in the gameplay view and why, plus frame timing and how many black-flash causes it blocked. Red lines are errors. Copy all copies everything to send.
  • Buttons stop working: press B on the right controller. The mod also does this by itself when the page loses focus.
  • Aim drifts or overshoots: it learns your in-game mouse sensitivity during the first few seconds of aiming. Sweep your right hand left and right once after entering VR.
  • Stutter: lower the headset resolution above, copy again, and replace the bookmark's URL.
  • Reloading the page removes the mod. Tap the bookmark again after each reload.
